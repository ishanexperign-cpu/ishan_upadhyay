Quiet Cairn

Stack stones on a sand garden. Each stone must differ from the one beneath it. The tallest cairn left standing wins.

  • Stones are 1 to 5 ft tall, and you can resize one before dropping it. Arches, rings and stars only touch where they touch.
  • The soft green glow shows roughly where the next stone is safe.
  • Rest your tower to bank its height. If it topples, only what still stands counts.
