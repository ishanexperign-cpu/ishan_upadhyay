A hand-tracked reflex game for up to ten people. The colours run while your palm is open. Close your fist the instant the screen matches the disc.

Five levels

Three attempts at each speed, then the loop gets faster. In Ladder, levels 4 and 5 split the screen for both hands.

A hit scores 100 to 150 points by precision. One slot off scores 25. Both hands on target adds 100. Speed multiplies everything, with 5 colours a second as ×1. A full run is 15 attempts, and ties go to the steadier average timing.
